Music words, in plain English

Pitch
How high or low a note sounds.
Semitone
The smallest step on a piano: one key to the very next one.
In tune
Your note lands on the target pitch, close enough that they blend.
Flat · Sharp
Flat means a bit too low. Sharp means a bit too high.
Beat
The steady pulse you tap your foot to.
Rhythm
The pattern of long and short sounds laid over the beat.
Staff
Five lines where notes are written. Higher on the staff = higher pitch.
Major · Minor
Two flavors of chord. Major sounds bright; minor sounds darker.
